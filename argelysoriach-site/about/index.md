# Argelys Samuel Oriach

Argelys Samuel Oriach, also known as Argelys Oriach, is a Dominican-born systems architect, performance artist, and artistic director based in Crown Heights, Brooklyn. He is the principal of Seven Eighty Three (Lab 783) and the founder and artistic director of Wild Lake Studio, known as Wild-Lake.

He operates two laboratories: Seven Eighty Three (Lab 783), a venture foundry that births infrastructure and whole companies, and Wild Lake Studio (Wild-Lake), the research laboratory created within it, which guides developing and independent artists.

## Early life and education

Born in the Dominican Republic, Oriach was raised by a housekeeper and a facility maintainer, and learned in adulthood that his mother was a writer and his father a musician, though neither pursued their craft. By his own account, he taught himself 3D design software at age eleven to draft a house for his parents.

He attended Phillips Academy Andover and, in 2010, the National High School Institute (Cherubs) theatre program at Northwestern University, where he played Nick in a stage adaptation of *The Handmaid’s Tale*.

He obtained a bachelor’s degree in English from Wesleyan University, with coursework in studio art and critical theory. He identifies his senior thesis as *Breaking the circle of the plantation: Violence, affect and the ephemeral in Junot Díaz’s Afro-Caribbean literary imagination*, an examination of *The Brief Wondrous Life of Oscar Wao* through queer theory, performance studies, affect studies, and critical race theory. He also describes being initiated into the Eclectic Society, producing live shows and organizing cultural space there, and singing with the Ebony Singers.

## Career

Oriach worked in growth and brand marketing for about twelve years. He served as social media manager for Cynthia Nixon’s 2018 campaign for governor of New York. His [CV](/cv/) lists roles as Director of Marketing at VinePair, Lead, Brand Voice & Content Strategy at Kickstarter, and Lead, Organic Growth & AI Modernization at CookUnity.

He has described these corporate roles as an undercover durational performance: a calculated effort to build his life out of poverty while studying how complex systems scale from the inside out. He says that performance cycle has closed with a return to his body, breath, and voice. His biography states that he is still building his life out of poverty.

## Laboratories

He operates two laboratories: Seven Eighty Three (Lab 783), a venture foundry that births infrastructure and whole companies, and Wild Lake Studio (Wild-Lake), the research laboratory created within it, which guides developing and independent artists.

[Seven Eighty Three](https://seveneightythree.com/) engineers automated infrastructure for executive leadership. [Wild Lake Studio](https://wildlakestudio.com/) formalizes what Oriach describes as a fifteen-year independent practice of guiding twelve or more developing and independent multidisciplinary artists. He identifies New York, Tampa, and Santiago as locations of artists supported by the studio. His central offering to artists is his undivided attention.

According to Oriach, Wild Lake Studio takes no ownership or percentage of an artist’s work. Its services are paid through a Stewardship Subscription, a retainer, or a fixed project fee. He describes a cross-subsidy model in which established artists fund capacity for rising artists.

## Artistic practice

Oriach describes performance as the governing method of his practice, with writing, voice, movement, design, and corporate roles as its forms. He is trained in the Alexander Technique. He characterizes his prose as a maximalist, automatic, and lyrical stream-of-consciousness style.
