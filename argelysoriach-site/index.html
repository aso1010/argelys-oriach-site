Argelys Samuel Oriach Systems Architect, Artist, and Auteur

About

Argelys Samuel Oriach is a systems architect, artist, and auteur constructing language for the page, stage, and screen.

A method actor trained in the Alexander Technique, he has spent two decades decoding the mechanics of human attention by fully inhabiting roles across corporate infrastructure, artistic performance, and institutional leadership. Born in the Dominican Republic, he reverse-engineered his life out of poverty through a rigorous study of human optics—mastering how people look, listen, and affect one another. This foundation fueled a twelve-year trajectory steering high-density organic growth engines before pivoting to architect backend applications and sovereign digital frameworks from first principles.

He operates across two primary laboratories: Seven Eighty Three, an independent systems design practice engineering narrative environments and automated infrastructure for executive leadership and cultural institutions, and Wild-Lake, an existential mechanics studio, A&R venture, and artists agency providing high-conviction mentorship and structural stewardship for serious performers and creators. Operating below the query layer to dictate how information and artistic intent are processed, his practice treats structure as a direct conduit for understanding rather than manipulation, redefining attention as a profound systemic responsibility.

As a writer, he applies this same structural rigor to narrative. He is the author of the digital short This Is Not a Love Story and is currently writing a one-act play alongside his debut autofiction novel, Graduation.
