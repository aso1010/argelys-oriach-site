04 // Background

Argelys Samuel Oriach

Argelys Samuel Oriach is an artist, creative director, actor, vocalist, writer, and systems architect.

Born in the Dominican Republic, he was raised by a mother who is a homemaker and writer and a father who is a vocalist and mechanical engineer working in maintenance.

At eleven, he taught himself 3D design software to draft his parents’ home. An architect later used that file for construction. He attended Phillips Academy Andover and earned a degree in English from Wesleyan University.

His work spans sixteen years of performance practice and twelve years building organic growth systems. He served as Cynthia Nixon’s social media manager during her 2018 campaign for governor of New York.

Trained in the Alexander Technique, he works across body, voice, text, and organizational structure. His current writing includes a pilot, a one-act play, and the autofiction novel Graduation.
