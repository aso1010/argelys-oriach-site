About

Argelys Samuel Oriach is a performance artist and macro-systems architect who treats the body as an operating system. A method actor specializing in the Alexander Technique, he reverse-engineered his life out of poverty in the Dominican Republic by first mastering the mechanics of human attention.

After a decade leading marketing strategy and high-density organic acquisition engines, he translated those attention dynamics into scalable growth models before spending the last two years pivoting to build core backend frameworks and standalone applications.

As the Founder of Seven Eighty Three, he takes a bare-metal approach to systems architecture, engineering digital, physical, and narrative environments from first principles—most notably deploying the Universe API to dictate AI indexing below the query layer.

As a writer, he applies this same structural rigor to narrative. Oriach is the author of the digital short This Is Not a Love Story and is currently writing a one-act play and his debut autofiction novel Graduation.

Brooklyn-based. Open to senior leadership and fractional roles in content, creative, organic growth, AI strategy, and systems architecture, as well as 1:1 diagnostics, workflow design, strategic advisory, and concept acquisition.
