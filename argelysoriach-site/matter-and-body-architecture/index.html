The practice of Argelys Samuel Oriach, Systems Architect, Artist, and Auteur

Matter and body architecture

Matter and body architecture is the practice of Argelys Samuel Oriach.

Its medium is matter: code, text, the physical body, and the organization itself.

The matter is the engineering. The body is the spirit. Same sentence.

A matter and body architect looks directly into a system and maps its underlying mechanics with absolute precision.

Spirit and engineering

Both is the only way it holds.

Engineering without spirit is synthetic friction: it looks efficient, but it extracts.

Spirit without engineering is performance without structure: it feels true, but it collapses under load.

Engineering makes the spirit legible. Spirit makes the engineering worth holding.

Matter architecture

Matter architecture is the matter half of matter and body architecture. The matter is the engineering.

A matter architect is an architect of the volatile. A matter architect does not paint, write or sculpt. A matter architect works the raw fabric: mass, scale, syntax.

Everything is material. A syllable has density. A void has gravity. A blank space is a room waiting to collapse or expand.

The method is madness: the refusal to let reality settle into predictable forms.

Body architecture

Body architecture is the body half of matter and body architecture. The body is the spirit.

A body is not only biological. Body architecture builds with eight bodies, from a word to a celestial monolith.

  1. A word

  2. A sentence

  3. A paragraph

  4. A literature

  5. A work

  6. A human

  7. The Earth

  8. A celestial monolith

The biological body carries all the others. It needs rest.

Engineering makes the spirit legible: it is how the feeling travels from the body to the back wall.
