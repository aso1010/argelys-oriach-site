Four brand platforms for four companies, 2025 to 2026. One rebrand concept.

For each one I originated the idea, wrote the line, set the visual system, and carried it to every surface: the site, the motion, the social set, the print and the deck.

The direction, the writing and the design are mine. The production is AI-assisted: I build with Claude and Codex, and I deploy what I build.

01

Banana Republic

Wayward type. One cut.

Rebrand concept · Independent · Not affiliated with Banana Republic or Gap Inc.

Role
Author. Concept, identity and web
Dates
October 2026
Page
argelysoriach.com/banana-republic
Scope
Wordmark, applications, homepage, case study

A speculative rebrand of a clothing retailer named after an insult. The name stays. The type carries what the name does.

Every letter leaves the line. The word is Saidiya Hartman’s: wayward. The one R in the name is cut, as wide as the gap between letters. It is the first letter of REPUBLIC, and in October 1937 the army of the Dominican Republic carried out the Parsley Massacre.

On the page the letters wander on their own clocks, and the R trills.

The homepage is re-architected too: one hero, six departments, and Women and Men as a setting rather than a fork.

Seven Eighty Three

Figure and ground. Nothing in excess.

Role
Chief Systems Architect & Founder
Dates
July 2026 to present
Domain
argelysoriach.com
Scope
Wordmark, emblem, site, motion, card, social, publishing

An identity for an independent systems design practice, and for my own name.

There is one typeface, Archivo. The name is set in three lines, each stretched to the same width, so it reads as a slab. Spacing follows the Fibonacci sequence. Line height is 1.618. There is no gradient, no shadow and no rounded corner.

There is one motion. The letters are thrown and land. Then the slab turns, and the name reads on both faces.

Mad Matter. Artist statement, published as a page and a seven-slide Instagram carousel

Method

How the work is made.

Engineering makes the spirit legible. Spirit makes the engineering worth holding.

  1. The line first

    The platform is one sentence before it is a design. If the sentence does not hold, nothing built on it will.

  2. The brief and the idea together

    I write the strategy and the concept as one document. The brief is shaped, not answered.

  3. One system

    One typeface, one ratio, one ground. Every surface inherits them, so the brand stays coherent across products.

  4. Every surface

    Site, motion, social, print and deck. I hold the idea from the first draft to the live deployment.

  5. Direction is mine. Production is AI-assisted

    I direct, write and design. Claude and Codex do the production. I check every frame and every line before it ships.

  6. State what is true

    A proposal is labeled a proposal. A hypothesis is labeled a hypothesis. The work says what it is.
