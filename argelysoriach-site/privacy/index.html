Privacy

What this site collects, and what it does not

01 //Summary

This site collects very little. It has no accounts, no advertising. Google Analytics measures visits and may set cookies, as described below.

02 //Analytics

The home page uses Cloudflare Web Analytics (opens in a new tab) to count visits and measure page speed. Cloudflare states that it does not use cookies or local storage to collect these measurements, and does not fingerprint visitors. Every page also loads the Google tag (Google Analytics), which counts visits and may use cookies to do so. Google describes how it handles that data in its own privacy policy.

03 //Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare receives the address and browser details of each request so that it can send the page.

04 //Fonts, images and films

All of them are served from this site. No third-party font, tracker or embed is loaded.

05 //The intake form

The intake form does not send anything to a server. When you press Draft the email, it opens your email app with a message addressed to argelys@seveneightythree.com. Nothing is sent until you send it from your email app.

If you send it, I receive your name, your email address, and what you wrote. I use it to reply and for nothing else. I keep it in my email account until I delete it. I do not sell it or share it. To have a message deleted, write to the same address.

06 //Light and dark setting

The story page remembers whether you chose the light or the dark setting. It keeps that choice in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

07 //Other sites

Links to LinkedIn, Paragraph, Wild-Lake, Communal Foundation, Seven Eighty Three and CookUnity lead to sites with their own policies.
